Who We Are

One Unified Partner From Material Supply to Final Handover

Al Maharat Al Alamiya operates as a fully integrated building materials supplier, architectural fabricator, and contracting enterprise. By uniting regional wholesale trading branches with dedicated manufacturing workshops and specialized construction teams, we eliminate multi-vendor delays—ensuring seamless quality control, competitive pricing, and on-schedule delivery across Qatar.

01 / TRADING & SUPPLY

Aluminum & uPVC Trading

Direct wholesale and retail supply of premium architectural profiles, European hardware, and accessories across our three regional branches.

02 / FENESTRATION

Aluminum & uPVC Fabrication

Custom engineering, manufacturing, and installation of thermal-break doors, windows, structural curtain walls, and modern kitchens.

03 / ARCHITECTURAL GLAZING

Glass Trading & Processing

High-performance single, double-glazed insulated units (IGUs), tempered safety glass, and custom architectural glazing fabrication.

04 / METALWORKS

Steel & Wrought Iron Works

Structural steel warehouses, bespoke entrance gates, laser-cut decorative mashrabiya panels, and architectural handrails.

05 / BESPOKE WOODWORK

Carpentry & Joinery

Solid wood and veneer doors, custom walk-in wardrobes, cabinetry, pergolas, and CNC-detailed interior decor.

06 / CIVIL & FINISHES

Construction, Gypsum & Painting

Civil contracting, modern gypsum ceiling systems, decorative cladding, and industrial spray coatings for metal, wood, and buildings.
