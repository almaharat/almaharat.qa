About Al Maharat Al Alamiya

Building excellence across Qatar through integrated material supply, specialized workshops, and disciplined engineering execution.

Our Story & Structure

Building Excellence Through Integrated Capabilities

Founded in 2012, Al Maharat Al Alamiya has grown into one of Qatar’s most versatile architectural fabrication, trading, and contracting enterprises. Rather than operating as a single-trade contractor, our organization brings together an extensive supply chain, specialized manufacturing workshops, and skilled site installation teams under one roof.

From supplying raw uPVC and aluminum profiles through our branches in the Doha Industrial Area, Al Khor, and Al Shamal, to fabricating custom glass, steel, and wood elements in our dedicated Al Wukair and Industrial Area facilities, we provide developers, main contractors, and private villa owners with a reliable, single-source partner.

Our in-house engineering team leverages modern AI-assisted design workflows alongside trusted specialist partners for intricate CNC detailing—ensuring every piece is fabricated, painted, and installed to exacting standards.

Our Vision

To be Qatar’s most trusted and comprehensive partner in architectural fabrication, building materials trading, and contracting—recognized for engineering reliability, modern craftsmanship, and seamless project delivery.

Our Mission

To empower our clients with superior building materials, precision-engineered fabrication, and disciplined site execution—combining deep regional industry expertise with modern technology to deliver lasting value on every project.

Why Choose Al Maharat Al Alamiya
