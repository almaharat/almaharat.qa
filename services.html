Capabilities & Specialized Divisions

Seven integrated operating divisions covering wholesale material trading, specialized workshop fabrication, surface finishing, and civil construction.

DIVISION 01

Aluminum & uPVC Profiles Trading

Serving contractors, fabricators, and developers across Qatar through our primary hub in the Doha Industrial Area and regional branches in Al Khor and Al Shamal.

  • Architectural aluminum profiles in multiple gauges and anodized/powder-coated finishes
  • Weather-resistant, energy-efficient uPVC profile systems
  • Complete range of European and international locks, hinges, handles, gaskets, and accessories
  • Bulk sourcing directly from international manufacturers with rapid branch-to-site logistics
DIVISION 02

Aluminum & uPVC Fabrication

Precision fabrication and on-site installation of custom fenestration and architectural facade systems tailored to Qatar’s demanding climate.

  • Thermal-break and standard aluminum doors, sliding systems, and casement windows
  • High-insulation, soundproof uPVC doors and windows
  • Structural glass curtain walls, commercial storefronts, and skylights
  • Custom-engineered modern aluminum kitchens and cabinetry
DIVISION 03

Glass Trading & Fabrication Factory

Dedicated glass processing and supply facility delivering high-performance architectural, safety, and decorative glazing solutions.

  • Single and double-glazed insulated glass units (IGUs) for solar and thermal control
  • Tempered safety glass, laminated security glass, and acoustic glass
  • Frameless glass office partitions, shower enclosures, balustrades, and shopfronts
  • Precision custom cutting, edge polishing, and site glazing installation
DIVISION 04

Steel & Wrought Iron Fabrication Workshop

Combining heavy structural durability with refined architectural aesthetics for industrial, commercial, and residential developments.

  • Custom wrought iron and steel main entrance gates and boundary fencing
  • Architectural staircases, handrails, and balcony balustrades
  • Decorative laser-cut metal screens, mashrabiya panels, and cladding (fabricated, finished, and installed in-house)
  • Heavy industrial structural steel warehouses and sheds with full Qatar Baladiya and Kahramaa compliant handovers
DIVISION 05

Carpentry & Custom Joinery Workshop

Skilled woodworking craftsmanship delivering bespoke architectural joinery and interior woodwork.

  • Solid wood, pressed, and natural veneer main entrance and interior doors
  • Custom walk-in closets, built-in wardrobes, and storage cabinetry
  • Decorative wall paneling, wooden pergolas, and custom CNC-detailed decor elements
  • Precision assembly, fine staining, and protective wood finishing
DIVISION 06

Industrial & Architectural Painting Division

Specialized surface preparation and spray coating services ensuring long-lasting corrosion protection and flawless aesthetic finishes.

  • Protective industrial coatings and decorative finishes for structural steel and wrought iron
  • Fine wood staining, lacquer, and polyurethane spray finishes for bespoke joinery
  • Interior and exterior architectural painting for residential villas and commercial buildings
DIVISION 07

Construction, Gypsum & Interior Finishing

End-to-end civil contracting and interior transformation managed by experienced site engineers and supervisors.

  • General building construction, structural modifications, and renovation works
  • Modern suspended gypsum board ceilings, bulkheads, and decorative lighting coves
  • Gypsum wall partitions and custom interior architectural features
  • Complete residential and commercial interior finishing packages
