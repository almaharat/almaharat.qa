Our Divisions

Six Integrated Architectural & Construction Divisions

From regional profile and hardware distribution to custom workshop manufacturing and final site installation.

DIVISION 01 / TRADING & SUPPLY

Aluminum & uPVC Trading

Direct wholesale and retail distribution of architectural aluminum profiles, uPVC systems, accessories, and European hardware across our branches in Doha Industrial Area, Al Khor, and Al Shamal.

  • Architectural Aluminum Extrusions & Accessories
  • uPVC Window & Door Profile Systems
  • Locks, Hinges, Rollers, Sealants & Fittings
DIVISION 02 / FENESTRATION WORKSHOP

Aluminum & uPVC Fabrication

Custom engineering, fabrication, and site installation of fenestration and kitchen systems from our GSW Al Wukair Logistics Park workshop.

  • Thermal-Break & Standard Aluminum Doors and Windows
  • uPVC Doors, Windows & Sliding Systems
  • Curtain Walls, Shopfronts & Aluminum Kitchens

View Aluminum & uPVC Workshop

DIVISION 03 / ARCHITECTURAL GLAZING

Glass Trading & Processing

Supply and precision processing of architectural and safety glass for commercial facades, interiors, and fenestration packages.

  • Double-Glazed Insulated Glass Units (IGUs)
  • Tempered, Laminated & Acoustic Safety Glass
  • Shower Enclosures, Mirrors & Glass Partitions
DIVISION 04 / METALWORKS WORKSHOP

Steel & Wrought Iron Works

Structural steel fabrication and bespoke architectural metalworks manufactured at our dedicated GSW Al Wukair facility.

  • Structural Steel Sheds, Warehouses & Mezzanines
  • Custom Entrance Gates, Fencing & Stair Handrails
  • CNC Laser-Cut Decorative Mashrabiya & Screens

View Steel & Wrought Iron Workshop

DIVISION 05 / JOINERY WORKSHOP

Carpentry & Joinery

Bespoke architectural woodwork, doors, and interior cabinetry crafted at our GSW Al Wukair joinery workshop.

  • Solid Wood, Veneer & Fire-Rated Wooden Doors
  • Custom Walk-In Wardrobes, Kitchens & Vanities
  • Wall Cladding, Pergolas & CNC Interior Woodwork

View Carpentry & Joinery Workshop

DIVISION 06 / CIVIL & FINISHES

Construction, Gypsum & Painting

Complete civil execution, interior gypsum systems, and protective industrial spray coatings for commercial and residential buildings.

  • Civil Contracting, Maintenance & Fit-Out Works
  • Modern Gypsum Board Ceilings & Partitions
  • Industrial Spray Coatings for Metal, Wood & Facades
