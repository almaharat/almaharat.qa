About Al Maharat Al Alamiya

Building trust, technical precision, and integrated delivery across Qatar's construction and architectural sectors since 2012.

OUR STORY

Over a Decade of Delivery in Qatar

Founded in 2012, Al Maharat Al Alamiya has grown into a multi-disciplinary enterprise serving main contractors, consultants, real estate developers, commercial facilities, and residential clients throughout Qatar.

By combining regional wholesale trading with dedicated fabrication workshops and site contracting teams, we give clients a single, accountable partner from material selection to final installation.

VISION & STANDARDS

Unified Quality From Workshop to Site

Our goal is to set a practical, dependable benchmark for architectural fabrication, finishing materials, and execution reliability across Qatar.

  • Direct sourcing and regional distribution across Doha, Al Khor, Al Shamal, and Al Wukair
  • In-house fabrication across aluminum, uPVC, glass, steel, wrought iron, and carpentry
  • Engineering review and modern visualization to align drawings with site realities
